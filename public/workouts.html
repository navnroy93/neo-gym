Concentration & Preacher Arm Protocol
# Exercise Sets × Reps Coaching Form & Target
01 Standing Dumbbell Biceps Curl 3 sets × 10–12 reps Strict standing posture. Supinate wrists at the apex and control a 2-second eccentric lowering tempo.
02 EZ-Bar Curl 3 sets × 8–12 reps Ergonomic cambered bar relieves wrist strain. Keep elbows tucked tight to your torso without swinging.

COACHING ESSENTIALS

TRAINING GUIDELINES

Directly from the NEO GYM master curriculum. Adhere to these principles to maximize your physique results while keeping injury risk minimal.

Rest Between Sets

Rest 2–3 minutes after demanding compound lifts and around 60–90 seconds for most isolation exercises and core work. Adjust as needed for recovery.

Warm-up Protocol

Start with 5–10 minutes of easy cardio and perform movement-specific warm-up sets before heavy compound lifts to lubricate joints and prime neurological pathways.

Progressive Overload

When all prescribed reps can be completed with good form, increase the load gradually rather than sacrificing technique. Consistency in progressive tension is key.

Beginner Adjustment

Beginners may start with 2 working sets per exercise and build volume gradually as technique, neuromuscular coordination, and recovery improve over time.

Recovery Standards

Take rest or active-recovery days as needed. Avoid training through sharp joint pain, and seek qualified certified guidance for injuries or medical limitations.

Personalisation & Note

These are example plans, not medical advice. Exercise choices are adaptable and are not strict gender requirements. Adjust volume for experience, goals, and equipment.
